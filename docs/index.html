WhatsApp Photo Saver

Automatically saves photos from the WhatsApp groups you choose — straight to a folder on your own computer. No account sharing, no cloud storage.

Windows 10 / 11 Free · MIT licence 100% local data
1

Install Node.js (only if you don't have it)

Download the LTS version, install it with all defaults, then come back. The setup below checks automatically and opens this page for you if it's missing.

Get Node.js (nodejs.org)
2

Download the installer

One small file (setup.bat). It downloads the app, installs everything and starts it — you'll just double-click it in the next step.

⬇ Download setup.bat
3

Double-click setup.bat

Sit back — it can take a few minutes on first run (it downloads the app and its browser engine). Your web browser will open at http://localhost:3000 when it's ready.

4

Link WhatsApp once

In the app, go to WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device on your phone and scan the QR code shown. Then tick the groups you want to watch — photos start arriving in your downloads folder automatically.

What stays on your own computer — always

  • Your WhatsApp login (a QR-linked session on this PC only — unlink any time from your phone)
  • Every downloaded photo, in the app's downloads folder (you pick the folder)
  • Your settings and the list of groups you selected

Nothing is uploaded to any server. The app talks directly between your PC, the website and WhatsApp.

Heads-up: this unofficial automation may violate WhatsApp's Terms of Service — use it with your own account, at your own risk. If WhatsApp adds a built-in download or an official API for this, prefer that.
